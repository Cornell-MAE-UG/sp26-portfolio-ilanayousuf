**Problem Statement and Objective:**

Find the location of maximum elastic deflection in the handles of the nutcracker and choose a “beam” design such that the vertical elastic deflection is below 2% of its length and is the most mass-efficient possible


**Free Body Diagram**

![FBD]({{ "assets/images/nccs-fbd.jpg" | relative_url }}){: width="600px" }


**Approach to the problem:**

Plan:
1) Find an equation for the deflection of AB using FBD and boundary conditions    
2) Solve for maximum deflection of AB, where y' = 0    
3) Find the maximum deflection of BC, at C    
4) Compare deflection from (2) and deflection from (3) to find overall maximum deflection      
5) Using Young's Modulus of a typical material (Aluminum 6601-T6), calculate maximum I of beam to satisfy constraints    
6) Calculate dimensions of most efficient cross section (I Beam)

**Design Usability:**

A handle height/diameter of 0.5 to 1 inch is typical for a kitchen tool such as a nutcracker. Additionally, the beam's cross section in this design maximizes efficiency, ensuring that the tool is lightweight enough for usability.
